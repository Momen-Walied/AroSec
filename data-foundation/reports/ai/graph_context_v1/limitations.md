# Limitations

- **Star graph**: one hub owns ~99.6% of edge-windows; 2-hop = whole graph. Structural features are dominated by hub membership; always read hub/non-hub strata.
- **No attack ground truth** on UNSW: structural scenarios are evaluation-only and are NOT attacks.
- **Homogeneous graph**: no services/VLANs/users/processes; graph context cannot exploit heterogeneous structure yet.
- **Scenario graphs are synthetic**: absolute scores are indicative, not production.
- Graph context is **independent evidence**, never fused with Edge/Node; no combined security score exists.
